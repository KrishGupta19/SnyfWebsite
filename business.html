The Case for Snyf

What Changes
When You Partner

Most venues lose revenue to friction — slow ordering, fake reviews, zero data. Snyf removes all three.

Before Snyf

Customers wait.
Staff get overwhelmed.

Manual ordering slows down table turns. Staff spend time taking orders instead of delivering them. Reviews on Zomato and Google are anonymous, unverified and easily gamed. You have no idea what your regulars order, when they come, or why they don't come back.

After Snyf

Guests order.
You just deliver.

Every table has a QR. Guests order directly. The kitchen sees it in real time. Waiters deliver and confirm payment. Reviews are tied to actual orders — verified, structured, weighted. You get a live trust score and a guest intelligence layer that gets sharper every week.

For Your Guests

No app install.
No friction.

Guests scan a QR code, browse your full menu, add items and place the order — all in their browser. No app download, no account required. After the meal they rate their experience, and that rating feeds directly into your Snyf Trust Score on the Discover page.

For Your Team

Kitchen, waiter,
all in sync.

Café OS is the operating layer for your staff. Kitchen staff see orders the moment they're placed. Waiters get notified when items are ready. Manual orders can be placed from the waiter terminal. Everything works offline too — so a dropped WiFi connection doesn't stop service.

Getting Started

Live in Under
48 Hours

From sign-up to first order — the onboarding is simple and the team at Snyf handles the setup with you.

01

Apply and get onboarded

Submit your venue details — name, location, category and operating hours. The Snyf team reviews and activates your account within 24 hours. You get a Café OS login, your venue slug and a setup checklist.

02

Build your menu in Café OS

Log into Café OS and add your menu items — names, descriptions, prices, categories, photos. Set item availability (e.g. brunch only, evening only). Your menu goes live on the QR ordering page automatically.

03

Place your QR stands

Snyf generates a QR code for each table in your venue. Download the print file, print at your local shop, or order our matte black acrylic QR stands. Each QR links directly to your live menu with the table number pre-set.

04

Go live and start receiving orders

Open Kitchen OS on the kitchen screen and Waiter Terminal on your floor staff's phones or tablets. The moment a guest scans and orders, your team sees it. No training required — the UI is built for kitchens, not tech teams.

05

Watch your trust score grow

Every verified order generates a post-meal review. As reviews accumulate, your Snyf Trust Score builds — visible on your Discover listing and inside your Café OS dashboard. The score updates daily.
